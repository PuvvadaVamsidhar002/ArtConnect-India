# 🧵 ArtConnect India – Handcraft Heritage

ArtConnect India is a tech-enabled platform designed to empower traditional artisans by aggregating their products into a centralized, QR-authenticated, and story-rich digital marketplace — without taking over inventory or disrupting their existing systems.

## 🪡 Problem Statement

India is home to thousands of unique, traditional art forms passed down through generations. However, many artisans face:
- Low digital visibility
- Exploitation by middlemen
- Poor income and lack of direct market access

Customers often face:
- Lack of authenticity
- Unclear pricing
- Difficulty finding genuine handmade products

## ✅ Solution Overview

ArtConnect India is a **non-inventory aggregator platform** that:
- Onboards existing artisan websites across India
- Provides a unified browsing and ordering interface
- Uses **QR codes** for each product to show artisan identity, story, and price breakdown
- Forwards orders to respective partner platforms for fulfillment
- Promotes transparency and ethical buying

## 🌍 Key Features

- 🔗 Aggregation of partner artisan websites
- 📱 User-friendly web interface
- 📦 Decentralized order fulfillment
- 📖 QR-based storytelling for each product
- 📊 Real-time impact dashboard for artisan earnings and buyer engagement

## 📈 Goals

- Empower traditional artisans with fair income
- Preserve India's cultural craft heritage
- Create a trustworthy ecosystem for buyers and sellers
- Align with *Vocal for Local*, *ODOP*, and *Make in India*

## 🧩 Tech Stack (Planned)

- **Frontend:** React / Next.js
- **Backend:** Node.js + Express
- **Database:** MongoDB / Firebase
- **QR Code System:** Dynamic links to artisan profiles
- **Cloud & Hosting:** AWS / GCP
- **Integration APIs:** For partner websites and payment flow
